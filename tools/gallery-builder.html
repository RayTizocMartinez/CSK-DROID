Gallery Builder

Websites can't look inside folders by themselves, so the phone reads a list of your photos from js/gallery-index.js. This page makes that file for you. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere — it all happens in this browser tab.

  1. Put your photos in media/photos. Each subfolder becomes an album (album names and photos are sorted A–Z on the phone).
  2. Click the green button and choose your media/photos folder. (Your browser may ask "upload files?" — it isn't uploading, it's just reading names.)
  3. Click Download gallery-index.js and put it in the js folder, replacing the old one.
  4. Upload media/photos and js/gallery-index.js to GitHub.
